## 📝 Scriptural Meditation and Personal Narrative
During my meditation on **Doctrine and Covenants 58:26–28**, the phrase *"for the power is in them, wherein they are agents unto themselves"* stood out sharply. In the IT sector, it is easy to become passive—writing sloppy code or leaving security gaps because "the manager didn't specify it in the ticketing system." 

True spiritual progression demands that we use our technical knowledge as active agents. When I write software or build configurations, I am not just assembling logical syntax; I am stewarding an environment that affects human lives, data privacy, and organizational trust. Technical excellence, therefore, becomes a direct manifestation of my testimony and love for God's children.
